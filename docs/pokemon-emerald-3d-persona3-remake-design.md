# Pokémon Emerald 3D x Persona-Style Story Remake (100+ Hour Design)

## Vision
Build a 3D Pokémon Emerald remake that keeps Hoenn's exploration, gyms, and monster collection while adapting a social-calendar narrative inspired by modern school-life RPG structure.

Design targets:
- **Main story:** 45–55 hours
- **Side content:** 35–45 hours
- **Postgame & New Game+:** 20–40 hours
- **Total per completionist run:** 100+ hours

## Narrative Structure (Persona-Inspired, Hoenn-Compatible)

### Core Setup
- Player transfers to Littleroot and joins a new **Hoenn Ecology Club** (social hub).
- Night anomalies called **Rift Hours** emerge around meteorite activity linked to Rayquaza/Groudon/Kyogre mythology.
- A hidden dungeon layer called the **Abyssal Routes** appears during Rift Hours.
- Team Aqua and Team Magma become rival factions trying to weaponize Rift phenomena.

### Story Arc Split (12 Chapters)
1. Arrival, starter, first bonds, first Rift Hour.
2. Rustboro and Dewford arcs: learning combat + social systems.
3. Slateport chapter: faction politics and corruption mystery.
4. Mauville chapter: first major character awakening moment.
5. Meteor Falls chapter: reveal of Rift source.
6. Mt. Chimney chapter: dual-faction branch mission.
7. Petalburg/Fortree chapter: trust collapse and reconciliation.
8. Lilycove chapter: personal stakes for companion cast.
9. Mossdeep chapter: space center + cosmic threat reveal.
10. Sootopolis chapter: legendary crisis and calendar deadline.
11. Victory Road chapter: final social link resolutions.
12. Ever Grande + Sky Pillar finale with true-ending conditions.

### Endings
- Standard ending (league + crisis resolved).
- Good ending (high party bond thresholds).
- True ending (specific confidant max ranks + optional boss clear).

## 100-Hour Content Plan

## Main Progression (50 hours)
- 8 gym arcs redesigned as cinematic chapters with bespoke side objectives.
- Story boss battles include multi-phase encounters with environmental mechanics.
- Key rival/team battles have relationship-state variants.

## Social & Daily Life Layer (25 hours)
- Day split into time blocks: Morning / Afternoon / Evening.
- Activities consume time: training, crafting, social events, jobs, contests.
- **12 core confidants** with rank 1–10 stories, perks, and route-specific scenes.

## Side Content (20 hours)
- Contest circuit with performance stat builds.
- Safari restoration program (collect resources to repopulate zones).
- Secret base co-op ghosts (asynchronous challenge teams).
- Regional requests board (100 rotating side quests).

## Postgame + Replay Systems (30+ hours)
- Battle Frontier expanded into narrative gauntlets.
- New Game+ unlocks retained compendium, bond perks, and route forks.
- Episode Delta expanded into 10-hour epilogue campaign.

## Replayability Pillars

### 1) Route Divergence
- Midgame faction-alignment missions affect bosses, dungeon layouts, and available confidants.
- Three route flavors: Neutral, Aqua-leaning, Magma-leaning.

### 2) Team-Build Diversity
- Encounter tables rotate by season/weather anomalies.
- Optional "Tactics" mode emphasizes synergy and status strategy over level advantage.

### 3) Relationship Build Variety
- Confidant abilities are mutually exclusive in one run (limited evening slots).
- Romance/close-friend routes alter late-game support scenes and combat assists.

### 4) Endgame Challenge Modes
- Frontier rule sets: Monotype, Draft, Ironman, and No-Item ladders.
- Weekly seeded dungeon modifiers with leaderboard ghosts.

## System Design Requirements

### Combat
- 3D turn-based presentation with fast animations and skip toggles.
- Boss armor, weak-point turns, and scripted phase shifts.
- Double-battle emphasis for key narrative encounters.

### Calendar & Pacing
- 10-month in-game calendar with fixed milestone deadlines.
- Hard-fail prevention options for accessibility (deadline assist mode).
- "Free Week" buffers before major deadlines to reduce soft-lock pressure.

### Economy & Crafting
- Part-time jobs unlock crafting materials and social stat growth.
- Pokégear app includes quest logs, confidant map, weather forecast, and Rift predictions.

## Production Scope Recommendation

## Vertical Slice (6 months)
- 1 town hub, 2 routes, 1 dungeon, 2 gyms.
- 3 confidants to rank 5.
- 1 Rift Hour cycle and 1 multi-phase story boss.

## Full Production (24–30 months)
- Full Hoenn world remade in modular 3D zones.
- 12 major story chapters and 3 ending states.
- 100+ quest arcs and complete Frontier postgame.

## Success Metrics
- Median first-run playtime >= 80 hours.
- Completionist run >= 100 hours for >= 60% of surveyed players.
- NG+ start rate >= 35%.
- At least 4 viable team archetypes in high-difficulty endgame telemetry.
